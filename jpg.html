JPG and JPEG Images

What's a JPG? A .jpeg (Joint Photographic Experts Group), sometimes labeled as .jpg, is an image file format that uses lossy compression to reduce file size, but sacrifices transparency as a feature.


JPGs are most commonly used for photographs and images with complex color gradients. They are widely supported across all web browsers and devices, making them a popular choice for web developers, designers, and photographers. Below the images will be a comparison of JPGs and PNGs.
