GIF Media

What is a GIF? A .gif (Graphics Interchange Format) is an image file type that supports both animated and static images. It is widely used for its ability to display short animations and low-resolution video clips.


GIFs are most commonly seen in digital/online art applications, web design, and even social media. GIFs also support a limited color palette of 255, making them suitable for simple graphics or animations, but unsuitable for other more professional uses.
