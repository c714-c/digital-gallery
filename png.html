PNG Images

What is a PNG? A .png (Portable Network Graphics) is an image file type that supports lossless data compression, designed/used for web graphics and many other general applications. It retains high image quality while also supporting complex details, such as transparency.


PNGs are most presently seen in digital/online art applications, web design, and even screenshots from devices. PNGs also support over 16 million colors, easily allowing for detailed graphics or illustrations.
